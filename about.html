About Me

I am a passionate student, and this is my very first step into the world of web development.

Living in Moro, a small city, I worked with dedication to create something new that had never been done here before.

This project, Zaiqa Zoom, is the result of my effort, my learning, and my hard work.

Through this website, I want to bring ease and comfort for everyone with online food delivery services.

Although it started small, I am continuously working to improve it and will keep adding new features in the future.

Every page and every detail reflects my struggle and passion for learning and creating something valuable.

This is just the beginning of my journey — I will keep moving forward, keep learning, and keep building better things ahead.
